Assets of "Power Grid: High Voltage" (pghv)

Source: patryk3211/PowerGrid (https://github.com/patryk3211/PowerGrid), licensed under Apache-2.0.
hv_voltmeter.png (assets/pghv/textures/block/) is a modified derivative of PowerGrid's conductive_gauge.png:
only the andesite casing areas are recoloured to brass; dial, metal back plate, copper strip and terminals are unchanged.
The voltmeter models use PowerGrid's original gauge models (powergrid: namespace) as parents.

Cooling fan: the block, rotor and item models are original geometry that REFERENCE Create's textures at runtime
(create:block/fan_casing, fan_side, fan_blades, axis, axis_top), scaled to 2x2. No Create texture or model file is included
(Create assets are All Rights Reserved); Create is a required dependency. The pins reference PowerGrid's texture
powergrid:block/wire_connector (Apache-2.0) at runtime.
Brass transformer fan: uses the same Create textures as the andesite fan (create:block/fan_casing, fan_blades, axis, axis_top),
referenced at runtime and tinted brass via tintindex/BlockColor (no Create file copied or modified); sides reference
create:block/brass_casing; pins powergrid:block/wire_connector.
item/data_cable.png: 16x16 pixel art redrawn by hand after a Google Gemini design draft made for this addon
(owner's prompt). block/data_socket.png, block/data_socket_box.png, block/data_plug.png and block/data_cable.png
are original pixel art made for this addon. The three small connectors on the data socket box use the geometry of
Power Grid's wire_connector model (Apache-2.0) and reference powergrid:block/wire_connector at runtime.

Item eu_light_bulb and its look in light fixtures reuse Power Grid's light bulb models (powergrid:block/lamps/*,
Apache-2.0) by reference; nothing is copied.
In-wall devices (wall_box, wall_switch, outlet_eu, outlet_us) are original pixel art made for this addon.
House installation (house_connection_box, distribution_board, rcd, main_switch: textures house_connection_box.png,
board_door.png, board_frame.png, board_rail.png, board_modules.png) is original pixel art made for this addon. Inside the
distribution board, Power Grid's fuse holder and energy meter (incl. its needle) are drawn with Power Grid's own models
(Apache-2.0) by reference.

Sounds (assets/pghv/sounds/*.ogg, generated by tools/sounds/make_sounds.py):
switch, push button, breaker, RCD, module/fuse insert and main switch sounds are processed (pitched, resonance, layered)
real recordings of mechanical switches from "UI Audio" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 (public domain).
cooling_fan_loop.ogg and fuse_blow*.ogg are synthesised for this addon (modelled air noise, blade and motor tones;
arc pop and sparks; the fuse pop contains a short Kenney CC0 click as transient).
